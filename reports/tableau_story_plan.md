# Tableau Story Plan: Sepsis ICU Patient Analysis

## Story 1: Population Break-up

Goal: Understand the overall population distribution between sepsis and non-sepsis patients, and compare MICU vs SICU admission patterns.

### Tasks
1. Percentage of non-sepsis patients
2. Percentage of sepsis patients
3. Sepsis patients admitted to MICU
4. Sepsis patients admitted to SICU
5. Non-sepsis patients admitted to MICU
6. Non-sepsis patients admitted to SICU
7. Create final population break-up visualization

### Planned Visuals
- KPI cards for percentages and counts
- Donut chart for sepsis vs non-sepsis distribution
- Bar chart comparing MICU and SICU by sepsis status
